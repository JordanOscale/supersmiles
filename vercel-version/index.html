Is this safe?

It's smart to be cautious. Here's the honest version.

You've probably heard warnings about "dodgy super dental offers." Good — that scepticism protects you. We'd rather agree with the regulator than hide in the fine print.

Q. Is this even legal?

Yes. Compassionate Release of Super is a genuine program run by the ATO. It lets people access super early for specific medical and dental needs. We help you apply correctly — we don't bend any rules.

Q. Will I get judged?

No. This scheme exists precisely for people who can't pay another way. We've helped people in exactly your situation. No lectures, no pity — just a clear path and real people.

Q. What's the catch on cost?

The eligibility check is free, and there's no fee unless your super is successfully released. We never ask you to pay for your treatment upfront. You'll see every step.

Early withdrawals are taxed (commonly around 17–22%, depending on your age and circumstances) and reduce your retirement savings. We'll always be upfront about that before you commit.

Eligibility, in plain English

You may be eligible if…

  • You've been told you need major dental work.
  • You're in dental pain, discomfort or distress.
  • You (or a dependent) have no other way to pay.
  • You have some funds in your super.
  • You can get a GP letter and an itemised dental quote — we help with both.

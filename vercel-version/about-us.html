Our story

Built for the person putting it off.

Plenty of people are quietly in pain — a tooth that's been wrong for months, work they've been told they need, a kid who needs a dentist — and no obvious way to pay for it. They've been told "no" by banks and by Centrelink. A loan feels like a trap. So they wait.

Meanwhile, they have super. And there's a legal ATO program — Compassionate Release of Super — built for exactly this. The problem isn't the money. It's that the process feels like a maze designed to confuse, and the fear of getting it wrong keeps people stuck.

We do the heavy lifting so an anxious, in-pain person doesn't have to face the paperwork alone.

That's the whole job. Check eligibility, arrange the GP letter, get a compliant itemised quote, prepare and lodge the ATO application, and stay with you until the funds are released. Plain speak, not legal speak. Real people, not a call-centre script.

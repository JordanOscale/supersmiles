Behind the scenes

The full done-for-you process.

For the curious — here's everything we manage so you don't have to. You'll have a dedicated case manager across all of it.

Eligibility assessment

We confirm your situation fits the ATO's compassionate-release criteria for dental treatment.

Dentist communication & compliant quote

We liaise with a super-approved dentist (or your own) to get a detailed, itemised quote the ATO will accept.

Collect & check documents

We gather everything, including the GP/medical letter, and quality-check it before anything is lodged.

Submission-ready review

A multi-point review to catch issues that commonly cause delays or rejections.

Book the lodgement

We schedule a time that suits you to submit the application together.

Live lodgement call

A screen-shared call so you can see exactly what's being submitted — full transparency, nothing hidden.

Address & ID verification

We help you complete the ATO's identity and address checks correctly.

ATO approval

The ATO assesses the application (usually up to ~14 days for a complete file).

File & fund-release prep

We prepare the release paperwork for your super fund.

Submit to your super fund

Your fund processes the release (commonly ~5–10 business days after approval).

Funds received & treatment coordinated

The money lands with you; we help coordinate your dental appointment. Time to smile.

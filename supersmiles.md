What we do

Super Smiles is an Australian service that helps everyday Australians legally access their superannuation early — through the ATO's Compassionate Release of Super program — to pay for urgent dental treatment. It's a done-for-you service: we check eligibility, arrange the GP letter and itemised dental quote, prepare and lodge the ATO application, and support the client until their super is released. Service operates in Australia only. The eligibility check is free; the service fee is $600 for every $10,000 successfully released.

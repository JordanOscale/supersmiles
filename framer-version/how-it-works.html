Behind the scenes

The full done-for-you service

Those three simple steps sit on top of six we handle behind the scenes — so you don't have to think about any of it.

Step 01

Eligibility assessment

We check whether your situation fits the ATO's compassionate-grounds criteria.

Step 02

Dental quote

You get a fully itemised quote from your dentist — or one experienced with this process — and we tell you exactly what it needs to include. We never change what your dentist recommends.

Step 03

Medical reports

We help you obtain the two medical reports the ATO requires — from a GP and a dentist. The clinical opinion is always the practitioner’s own.

Step 04

Document check & review

We gather everything and double-check it carefully before anything is lodged.

Step 05

Prepare & lodge

We prepare your application and walk you through lodging it from your own myGov account on a screen-share. We never ask for your myGov login.

Step 06

Support until released

We stay with you through ATO approval until your super is released and your treatment's booked.

Straight talk on timing

No surprises. Here's the honest picture.

~14 days at the ATO

Assessment usually takes up to around two weeks. We can't speed up the ATO, but we can make sure your file doesn't bounce back.

5–10 days to release

After approval, your super fund typically releases the money within 5–10 business days, straight to you.

We’re not tax advisers

We handle the application, not advice. For anything about tax or your retirement savings, we’ll point you to a registered professional — and never pressure you. Early withdrawals are taxed and permanently reduce your retirement savings.

The program

ATO: Compassionate release of super

Super Smiles is a trading name of MEROITI, TUMAI (ABN 75 527 278 804). We help everyday Australians prepare and organise an application for the early release of super on compassionate grounds — the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) program — for dental treatment. We are not financial advisers, not the ATO, and not a tax or legal adviser; nothing on this site is financial, tax or legal advice. Eligibility and approval are decided solely by the ATO and your super fund — we cannot and do not guarantee any outcome. Accessing super early is taxed, reduces your retirement savings, and is intended as a last resort. Please consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice.

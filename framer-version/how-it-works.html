Straight talk on timing & tax

No surprises. Here's the honest picture.

~14 days at the ATO

Assessment usually takes up to around two weeks. We can't speed up the ATO, but we can make sure your file doesn't bounce back.

5–10 days to release

After approval, your super fund typically releases the money within 5–10 business days, straight to you.

Tax applies

Early withdrawals are taxed (commonly ~22% or your marginal rate) and reduce your retirement savings. We'll spell out the numbers before you commit — but we're not tax accountants or financial advisers, so this isn't advice. Please check with a registered tax professional before deciding.

The program

ATO: Compassionate release of super

Super Smiles helps prepare and lodge applications under the ATO's Compassionate Release of Super program. We are not financial advisers and this is not personal financial advice. Eligibility and approval are determined by the ATO and your super fund — we cannot guarantee any outcome. Accessing super early is taxed and reduces your retirement savings. Consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice if needed.

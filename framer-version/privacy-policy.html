Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here’s how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. Introduction

[Legal entity name] (ABN [ABN]), trading as “Super Smiles” (“we”, “us”, “our”), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). It applies to supersmiles.au and our Services.

2. The information we collect

We collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide our Services, including:

  • Contact details — your name, email, phone number and general location.
  • Application information — details about your circumstances, your superannuation, and the dental treatment you need.
  • Sensitive information — including health information (such as your dental or medical condition and practitioner reports). We only collect sensitive information with your consent and where it is necessary for our Services.
  • Technical information — collected automatically when you use the Site, such as your device, browser, IP address and usage, via cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

We never collect or ask for your myGov login details.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you — for example, when you use our chat, complete a form, book a consultation, or communicate with us — and, with your authority, from third parties such as your dentist or doctor.

4. Why we collect it and how we use it

  • To provide our Services and prepare and organise your application.
  • To communicate with you and respond to your enquiries.
  • To improve the Site and our Services.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We only use your information for the purposes for which it was collected, related purposes you would reasonably expect, or as otherwise permitted by law or with your consent.

5. When we disclose it

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • the ATO and your superannuation fund, with your authority, as part of your application;
  • your dentist or doctor, with your consent, to arrange the required documents;
  • service providers who help us operate (for example, IT, hosting, booking and communication providers), under confidentiality obligations;
  • others where required or authorised by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Direct marketing

We may send you information about our Services where permitted. You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

7. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside Australia. Where they do, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the APPs.

8. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law, then take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

9. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on the Site. For details and how to manage them, see our Cookie Policy.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you. To do so, contact our Privacy Officer at [Email]. We will respond within a reasonable time, and if we refuse we will explain why.

11. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us first at [Email] and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

12. Children

Our Services are intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is indicated by the “Last updated” date above.

The program

ATO: Compassionate release of super

Super Smiles is a trading name of [Legal entity name] (ABN [ABN]). We help everyday Australians prepare and organise an application for the early release of super on compassionate grounds — the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) program — for dental treatment. We are not financial advisers, not the ATO, and not a tax or legal adviser; nothing on this site is financial, tax or legal advice. Eligibility and approval are decided solely by the ATO and your super fund — we cannot and do not guarantee any outcome. Accessing super early is taxed, reduces your retirement savings, and is intended as a last resort. Please consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice.

Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage; in this policy we refer to them all as “cookies”.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to operate and improve supersmiles.au. The types we use include:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the Site to function (for example, security and basic navigation). These can’t be switched off in our systems.
  • Performance and analytics — help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it.
  • Functionality — remember choices you make to give you a better experience.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use — for example, web fonts, an embedded booking tool, or analytics providers. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing your cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings, and set your browser to refuse some or all cookies. If you disable certain cookies, parts of the Site may not work properly. See your browser’s help pages for guidance.

5. Consent

By continuing to use the Site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy, except where your consent is otherwise required and obtained.

6. More information

For how we handle the personal information we collect, please read our Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The current version is indicated by the “Last updated” date above.

The program

ATO: Compassionate release of super

Super Smiles is a trading name of MEROITI, TUMAI (ABN 75 527 278 804). We help everyday Australians prepare and organise an application for the early release of super on compassionate grounds — the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) program — for dental treatment. We are not financial advisers, not the ATO, and not a tax or legal adviser; nothing on this site is financial, tax or legal advice. Eligibility and approval are decided solely by the ATO and your super fund — we cannot and do not guarantee any outcome. Accessing super early is taxed, reduces your retirement savings, and is intended as a last resort. Please consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice.

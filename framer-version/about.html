About Super Smiles

We turn super into real solutions — not distant dreams.

Too many Australians live with dental pain while their own money sits locked away in super, behind a process that feels built to confuse them. We exist to change that — one application at a time.

Our story

No loans. No credit cards. No judgment.

When you can't afford urgent dental work, the usual answers are a loan you can't service or a credit card you'll regret. We think there’s a better way: your own super, accessed the way the program allows.

The hard part was never the program itself — it was the paperwork. The forms, the medical letters, the back-and-forth with the fund. So we built a service that handles all of that for people — in plain English, and with respect at every step. Because needing help with your health isn't something to be ashamed of.

Who we are

A document-preparation & support service.

We help you gather, organise and check the paperwork for a Compassionate Release of Super application, and we support you while the ATO assesses it.

What we're not

We're not financial advisers, not tax or legal advisers, not the ATO, and not a dental or medical provider.

  • Your dentist makes the clinical decisions
  • The ATO decides your application
  • You lodge it from your own myGov account

We make the process clearer — we don't change the outcome.

Our vision

The program

ATO: Compassionate release of super

Super Smiles is a trading name of MEROITI, TUMAI (ABN 75 527 278 804). We help everyday Australians prepare and organise an application for the early release of super on compassionate grounds — the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) program — for dental treatment. We are not financial advisers, not the ATO, and not a tax or legal adviser; nothing on this site is financial, tax or legal advice. Eligibility and approval are decided solely by the ATO and your super fund — we cannot and do not guarantee any outcome. Accessing super early is taxed, reduces your retirement savings, and is intended as a last resort. Please consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice.

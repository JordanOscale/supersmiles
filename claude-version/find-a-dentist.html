For your dentist

What the ATO needs from them

Three straightforward documents — most practices can prepare them in a single appointment.

01

An itemised quote

Each procedure listed with its dental item number and cost, on the practice's official letterhead.

Required
02

A necessity statement

A short letter confirming the treatment is clinically necessary and shouldn't be delayed.

Most important
03

Practice details

The dentist's registration number and practice ABN — usually already on the letterhead.

Usually automatic

We send your dentist a clear one-page guide spelling out exactly what to include.

Eligible treatment

What dental work tends to qualify

Necessary, health-driven treatment qualifies far more readily than purely cosmetic work.

Implants & missing teeth

Implants, bridges and dentures that restore function and prevent bone loss.

Root canal treatment

Endodontic work to relieve pain and save an infected tooth.

Gum disease treatment

Periodontal care for gum disease affecting oral and general health.

Crowns & restorations

Crowns, inlays and major repairs for compromised teeth.

Extractions & surgery

Complex extractions, impacted wisdom teeth and oral surgery.

Some orthodontics

Braces or aligners where there's a documented functional or health need.

Purely cosmetic work won't qualify

Whitening, aesthetic-only veneers and cosmetic contouring aren't approved by the ATO — treatment has to address a genuine health need. Not sure which side yours falls on? Book a free call and we'll tell you honestly.

At the appointment

What to say to your dentist

"I need an itemised quote for an ATO super release"

This exact phrase tells your dentist precisely what kind of document to prepare.

Ask for dental item numbers

The ATO needs procedure codes — a plain description on its own isn't enough.

Request a one-paragraph necessity note

Have them confirm the treatment is clinically necessary. This matters most.

Everything on letterhead

Practice name, ABN and the dentist's signature — the ATO rejects documents without them.

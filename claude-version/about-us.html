About us

We exist to get Australians out of pain.

Too many people live with dental pain because the money to fix it is locked in their super and the paperwork to access it feels impossible. We make that pathway simple, honest and done-for-you.

Our mission

A clear path through a confusing system

The Compassionate Release of Super program is genuinely good policy — but it's buried in requirements that stop people right when they're least able to deal with them.

We built Super Smiles to be the opposite of that: plain English, a single dedicated contact, and a service that does the hard parts for you. We only charge when it works, so we're motivated to get it right.

The basics

Yes — if your dental treatment is medically necessary, you may be eligible under the ATO's Compassionate Release of Super program. The key words are "may be eligible": the ATO makes the final decision, and we help you present the strongest possible application.

Implants, crowns, oral surgery, extractions, root canals and reconstructive work may qualify when medically necessary. Purely cosmetic work like veneers or whitening generally doesn't — unless it has a genuine medical purpose, such as restoring function after trauma.

Generally: an itemised dental quote, a letter from a GP confirming the treatment is medically necessary, and a correctly submitted application. We help arrange all three — and the application is the part we do entirely for you.

Yes. You can apply to release your super for a dependent's dental treatment — a child or partner — with evidence of your relationship and their condition. We'll guide you through what's needed.

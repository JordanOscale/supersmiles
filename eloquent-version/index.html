Is this safe?

A real, legal program — not a loophole.

It's smart to be cautious about touching your retirement savings. So here's the honest version.

Compassionate Release of Super is an official Australian Taxation Office program. It exists specifically to help people pay for medically necessary treatment — including dental — when they have no other way. We simply help you apply for it, properly.

Why you can trust us

Named ATO scheme

It's the Government's Compassionate Release of Super program. We link you to the official ATO information so you can read it yourself.

No approval, no fee

We never touch your money
